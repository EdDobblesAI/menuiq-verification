What we measure
Each month we collect the online drink menus of bars and restaurants in the US and Canada and record every alcohol brand listed. In September 2026 we found 122,587 outlets with an alcohol menu online. 106,468 of them name at least one brand; the other 16,119 list drinks without naming a brand (“draft beer”, “house red”) or list only their own products: open doors for any brand. A typical branded menu lists 22 brands.

How to Read This Page

Menus — outlets whose menu lists at least one of your brands.
Share of menus — those outlets as a share of all 122,587 alcohol-serving outlets.
Menu listings — every line on a menu that names your brand (a bar listing two of your bourbons counts twice).
Menus without you — alcohol-serving outlets that carry none of your brands: your prospect list. For spirits companies, counted among the menus that list spirits.
This is menu presence, not sales volume or market share. September 2026 is our first published month; month-over-month change starts in October.
Methodology

Source. Menus are read from each outlet's own website. For 832 chain locations (0.7%), the chain's standard drink list is used instead of the location's own page.

Base. North America: 122,587 outlets with a September 2026 menu; 106,468 of them list at least one brand; mean 40.27 brands per branded outlet (median 22), cocktail ingredients excluded. Outlet universe: 142,034 venues, minus 6,429 excluded (closed, wrong or unverified website), 5,066 duplicate records and 155 cannabis dispensaries, plus 832 chain locations verified on Google Maps.

Brands and owners. 1,533 brands on 100 or more menus. Each brand's current owner was checked against a public source in September 2026; the current owner is applied to the whole period. Spelling variants of one brand are combined. Brands whose owner could not be confirmed are left out. Company search covers the 200 largest companies by listings; state detail covers the top 50 (US states).

Spirits. Includes liqueurs, aperitifs, bitters, fortified wine and sake.
